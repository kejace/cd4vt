    What are the terminal flags? ＋

    After the secondary peak is normalized to κ₀ = 1 and the unique maximum is at H, write Φ(p,q,h,l) = asin(p) + asin(qσ) − asin(σ) − π/2, with σ = √((1−l²)/(h²−l²)).

    The forward flag is Φ₊ = Φ(κ₁, κH−1, κH, κ−1). The backward flag is Φ₋ = Φ(κ−1, κH+1, κH, κ₁). The forward nonnegative case is taken first; the backward case then requires Φ₊ < 0. Equality belongs to the first-transition branches.
